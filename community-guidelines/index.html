Community Guidelines

Race Poke is an adults-only community for helping runners plan races with people they know.

Be respectful

Do not harass, threaten, shame, or target another person.

Be authentic

Do not impersonate people or use deceptive usernames or invitations.

Keep it relevant

Do not spam friend requests, invitations, reports, race activity, likes, or comments. Do not post malicious links.

Keep content appropriate

Profile photos and comments must not contain nudity, sexual content, graphic violence, hate, threats, harassment, or illegal material. Automated filtering supplements, but does not replace, human review.

Protect privacy

Do not post or misuse another person’s phone number or personal information.

Protect children

Race Poke strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including generated or altered depictions.

Use safety tools

Block users you do not want to interact with and report users, profile photos, or comments that violate these rules. Select Child safety concern for suspected CSAE or CSAM. Race Poke targets review of safety reports within 24 hours, removes prohibited access or material, preserves and reports evidence when legally required, and cooperates with lawful investigations.

Contact

Use Report on the relevant profile for urgent safety concerns, or email Race Poke Safety at support@racepoke.com. Never attach or redistribute suspected illegal material.

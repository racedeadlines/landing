Support

Need help with your account, a race reminder, contact discovery, an invitation, or a safety report? Include your username, app version, and a description of what happened. Never send an OTP code.

Email support@racepoke.com

Safety reports

For urgent safety reports, use Report on the relevant profile. We target a response within 24 hours.

Child-safety concerns

Select Child safety concern in the report form or email Race Poke Safety at support@racepoke.com. Never attach or redistribute suspected illegal material.

Account deletion

Follow the account deletion instructions to remove a Race Poke account and associated data.

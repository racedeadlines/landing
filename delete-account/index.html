Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Race Poke account from the mobile app.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Race Poke and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Permanently delete account.
  4. Request and enter the fresh SMS verification code.
  5. Confirm permanent deletion.

If you cannot access the app

Email support@racepoke.com from an address you can access and include your username. Support will verify account ownership before completing the request. Never send an OTP code by email.

What is deleted

Deletion immediately removes live profile data and photos, comments, likes, reminders, friendships, push-device data, and account access. Safety reports are de-identified when retention is necessary. Residual encrypted backups expire within 30 days, unless a legal preservation requirement applies to a specific record.
